Detailed Instructions for Confession

Steps of Confession

1. Examination of Conscience

Prayerfully review your life since your last confession. Be honest with yourself and God. Consider your thoughts, words, actions, and omissions.

2. Contrition

Be truly sorry for your sins. Sorrow should come from love of God above all else. Resolve to avoid sin in the future and to do penance.

3. Confession

Confess your sins to the priest honestly and completely. Do not hide any mortal sins. State the kind of sin and approximately how many times.

4. Satisfaction (Penance)

Accept the penance given by the priest and complete it as soon as possible. This helps repair the damage caused by sin and restores us to God's grace.

5. Absolution

The priest, acting in the person of Christ, absolves you from your sins. Listen carefully to the words of absolution and make the sign of the cross.

How to Go to Confession

  1. The priest will greet you.
  2. The penitent says: "Bless me, Father, for I have sinned." You then say how long it has been since your last Confession.
  3. The penitent tells the priest his sins simply and forthrightly.
  4. The priest will give you some advice and will assign you a penance.
  5. The penitent will next pray the Act of Contrition (see prayer below).
  6. The priest then gives the penitent absolution.
  7. The priest will then say: "Give thanks to the Lord, for He is good."
  8. The penitent responds: "His mercy endures forever." The priest will then dismiss you.

Prayer Before Confession

Come, Holy Spirit, enlighten my mind that I may clearly know my sins. Move my heart that I may be sincerely sorry for them, honestly confess them and firmly resolve to amend my life. Spirit of Wisdom, grant me to see the malice of sin and my ingratitude toward You, the all-loving God. Spirit of Fortitude, help me to make whatever sacrifice is needed to avoid sin in the future. Amen.
